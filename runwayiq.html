How it works

Your portfolio. Live data. Real expertise.

RunwayIQ connects your actual balances to live award availability and serves up plans you can act on today.

Log your transferable points — Amex MR, Chase UR, Citi TYP, Capital One, Bilt — and your loyalty programs. RunwayIQ knows exactly what you can fund and at what transfer ratio.
