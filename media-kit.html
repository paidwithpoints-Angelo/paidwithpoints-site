About

Turning Everyday Spending Into Extraordinary Travel

Paid With Points is a luxury travel education brand that teaches people how to turn everyday spending into business- and first-class flights, five-star hotels, and bucket-list experiences — without paying cash.

Founded by Angelo Minella, the brand simplifies complex points and miles strategies into actionable, real-world guidance. Known for credible, experience-backed education — not hype or unrealistic hacks.
